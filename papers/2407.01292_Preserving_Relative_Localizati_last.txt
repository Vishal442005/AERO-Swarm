VII. C ONCLUSION AND F UTURE W ORK
This paper proposed an active localization correction
system for the vision-based FoV-limited swarm. Compared
with previous works that utilize visual mutual observation in
relative state estimation, we designed an extra module, which
calculates appropriate actions for drones to actively execute
mutual observation, to solve the contradiction between mutual observation and environment observation that occurs in
the FoV-limited swarm.
In the future, the active mutual observation can be executed via path planning in addition to yaw planning to
avoid the loss of environment observation during mutual
observation.
