Title: Timebox-enhanced evolutionary algorithm for electric vehicle–drone routing problem with drone stations and time windows

--- ABSTRACT ---
This paper presents a futuristic logistics system where a combination of electric vehicle–drone tandems and drone stations are employed to serve customers within their specified time windows. Integrating time windows constraints represents a forward step in devising a routing model that resembles a real-world practice. However, the addition of time windows into a system with multiple transportation modes yields a challenging optimization problem with immense computational complexity and a tight feasible region. Existing approaches generally start by creating a random population or a dedicated solution that neglects the time windows, leading to a weak starting point that deteriorates the algorithms’ performance. To address this problem, we propose a timebox-enhanced adaptive memetic algorithm. In this algorithm, we introduce a timebox classification technique to classify customers based on their service deadlines. This technique is then utilized to create a feasible initial solution in the presence of time windows constraints and speed up the algorithm’s convergence. Then, the algorithm iteratively improves the initial population by decomposing the model into location-routing and scheduling subproblems and respectively solves them with a Q-learning-based adaptive memetic algorithm and a job stacking heuristic. Our experimental study demonstrates that the proposed approach can enhance both operational efficiency and energy efficiency, yielding 13%–37% improvement over alternative approaches in terms of the solution’s quality. Furthermore, the inclusion of the specialized initialization technique leads to a 21% improvement in the algorithm’s performance.
